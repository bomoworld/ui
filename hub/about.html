ABOUT BOMO

Travel is built on trust. BOMO is built around it.

BOMO is a travel platform that brings travelers and property partners together through clearer information, easier booking experiences, real stays, and meaningful post-stay participation.

For Travelers For Property Partners
WHAT IS BOMO?

More than finding a place to stay.

A great travel experience starts before check-in and continues after checkout. BOMO connects the important parts of that journey in one experience.

Travelers can discover properties, understand their options, book stays, manage trips, and share eligible experiences after completing a stay. Property partners can present their properties, manage rooms and rates, handle reservations, and connect with travelers throughout the stay journey.

The result is a more connected travel experience where better information supports better decisions for both travelers and property partners.

Our Mission

Make travel more connected, transparent, and rewarding.

BOMO's mission is to create a travel platform where travelers can make better-informed booking decisions, property partners can build stronger relationships with guests, and real travel experiences can continue creating value after checkout.

We aim to connect property discovery, booking, the actual stay, verified post-stay participation, and eligible rewards in one continuous travel journey.

Our Vision

A travel ecosystem powered by real stays and real experiences.

BOMO envisions a future where travelers are not only customers of tourism, but active participants in helping others discover places, understand properties, and make more confident travel decisions.

We want hospitality businesses of different sizes to have better opportunities to be discovered through useful information, authentic guest experiences, and technology that connects operations with the traveler journey.

OUR VISION FOR TRAVEL & TOURISM

Helping real travel experiences create more value.

Tourism grows when travelers can discover places with greater confidence and when hospitality businesses have better ways to communicate what makes their properties and destinations worth experiencing.

BOMO aims to contribute to a more connected tourism ecosystem by bringing travelers, accommodations, destinations, and real post-stay experiences closer together.

Support Hospitality Partners

Create More Meaningful Participation

TRAVELERS AS PARTICIPANTS

Stay. Share your experience. Create value.

BOMO is built around the idea that a traveler's experience can remain valuable even after the stay is over.

After an eligible completed stay, travelers may be able to use a Review Credit to submit a video review. Eligible approved participation may provide opportunities to earn rewards under applicable BOMO programs, terms, and eligibility requirements.

The goal is to create a more meaningful exchange: travelers share useful first-hand experiences, future guests gain additional insight, and property partners gain visibility through content connected to actual stays.
