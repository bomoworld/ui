BOOKING HELP

Cancellations & Refunds

Understand your booking's cancellation conditions, possible charges, refund eligibility, and what to do before cancelling a stay.

View My Bookings How to Cancel

Your booking's cancellation policy determines what applies.

Cancellation conditions may vary by property, room, rate plan, promotion, booking date, stay dates, and the time the cancellation request is made. Always review the conditions shown on your booking before confirming a cancellation.

START HERE

Before you cancel a booking

Review the cancellation conditions attached to your specific booking. The conditions shown during booking and in your booking details determine whether cancellation is free, subject to a charge, or non-refundable.

1. Review your booking

Open your booking details and locate the cancellation policy, applicable deadline, payment conditions, and any special restrictions.

2. Check the deadline

A booking may be cancellable without charge only before a specified date and time. Cancelling after the deadline may result in a partial or full cancellation charge.

3. Check your refund

If you already paid and a refund is due, the refundable amount and processing method will depend on your booking conditions and original payment method.

CANCELLATION TYPES

Common cancellation conditions

These are general examples. The conditions displayed on your actual booking take precedence.

Free cancellation

Cancel before the stated deadline

No cancellation charge may apply if the booking is cancelled before the deadline stated in the booking conditions.

Cancellation fee

A charge may apply after a deadline

Depending on the rate conditions, the charge may be a fixed amount, a percentage of the booking, or the cost of one or more nights.

Non-refundable

Payment may not be refundable after booking

A non-refundable rate generally does not provide a refund if you choose to cancel. Review the specific conditions before completing the original booking.

REFUNDS

What happens after a refundable cancellation?

If your cancelled booking is eligible for a refund, the amount and processing workflow will depend on the booking and payment details.

Refund amount

Any applicable cancellation charge may be deducted before the refundable amount is determined.

Original payment method

Where supported, refunds are generally processed through the applicable original payment channel.

Processing time

The time required for a completed refund to appear can vary depending on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or payment method.

Keep your confirmation

Keep your booking and cancellation details available if you need to contact support regarding a refund.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Cancellation and refund questions

Can I cancel every booking for free?

No. Free cancellation depends on the conditions attached to the specific booking. Some bookings may have a deadline, a cancellation fee, or a non-refundable condition.

Will I receive a full refund?

The refundable amount depends on your booking conditions, cancellation timing, payments already made, and any applicable cancellation charges.

What if I cannot cancel online?

Review your booking details for available support or property contact options. You can also use the BOMO Help Center for the appropriate support route.

Does cancelling a stay create a Review Credit?

No. Review eligibility is connected to qualifying completed stays. A cancelled booking does not represent a completed stay.

Need help with a specific booking?

Have your booking details available so the appropriate support route can identify the reservation and understand the applicable conditions.
