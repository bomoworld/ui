Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how BOMO may use cookies and similar technologies when you access or use our websites, applications, traveler services, property partner tools, and related platform services.

Effective: July 2026 Applies to BOMO Platform Services

About this policy

Cookies and similar technologies may support essential platform functions, remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve security, measure platform performance, and help BOMO understand how its services are used. The technologies used may vary depending on the BOMO service, device, browser, location, and features available to you.

01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that a website may store on your browser or device when you visit or use an online service. Cookies can help a platform recognize a browser or device, maintain a session, remember preferences, support security features, and understand how services are used.

BOMO may also use technologies that perform similar functions, including local storage, session storage, software development kits, pixels, tags, identifiers, and other technologies where applicable. In this policy, references to “cookies” may also include these similar technologies unless otherwise stated.

02

How BOMO may use cookies and similar technologies

BOMO may use cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, improve, and measure its platform and services.

  • Maintain authenticated sessions for travelers, guests, property partners, and authorized platform users.
  • Support account registration, login, verification, and security processes.
  • Remember selected preferences, settings, language, currency, or other interface choices where available.
  • Support booking, reservation, property management, and other platform workflows.
  • Help prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and suspicious activity.
  • Measure platform traffic, performance, reliability, and feature usage.
  • Diagnose errors and improve the usability and performance of BOMO services.
  • Support integrations and third-party services where required for a requested feature or platform function.
03

Categories of cookies and technologies

Depending on the services and features in use, BOMO may use the following categories.

Strictly Necessary Technologies

Required

These technologies are necessary for core platform operations and may be required to provide a service requested by the user. They may support login sessions, account security, booking workflows, fraud prevention, network management, and other essential functionality.

Because these technologies are required for essential services, disabling them through browser settings may cause parts of the BOMO platform to stop working correctly.

Preference and Functional Technologies

Where Used

These technologies may remember choices and settings to provide a more consistent experience. Examples may include interface preferences, selected language, currency, display settings, or other user-selected options.

Analytics and Performance Technologies

Where Applicable

These technologies may help BOMO understand how users interact with its services, including page visits, feature usage, navigation patterns, technical performance, and errors.

Information collected through analytics technologies may be aggregated or otherwise processed to improve platform performance, usability, and service development.

Advertising and Marketing Technologies

If Implemented

If BOMO uses advertising, campaign measurement, remarketing, or similar technologies, these technologies may help measure marketing effectiveness, understand campaign interactions, or provide more relevant promotional experiences.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential advertising or marketing technologies should be subject to appropriate notice, consent, or user controls.

04

Local storage and similar technologies

BOMO may use browser storage or similar technologies to store information locally on a device. These technologies may support interface settings, temporary application data, authentication workflows, performance, or other platform functionality.

Unlike traditional cookies, some browser storage technologies may not be controlled through standard cookie settings. Users may need to manage them through browser, device, or application settings.

05

Third-party services and technologies

Some BOMO features may rely on third-party technology providers, service providers, or integrations. Depending on the services implemented, third parties may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are loaded or used.

These services may include, where applicable:

  • Payment and transaction service providers.
  • Authentication and account verification providers.
  • Analytics and platform performance services.
  • Fraud prevention and security services.
  • Maps, location, or destination-related services.
  • Customer support and communications services.
  • Property, booking, channel, or hospitality integrations.
  • Marketing and campaign measurement services, if used.

Third-party practices

Third-party services may operate under their own privacy and cookie policies. BOMO encourages users to review the applicable policies of third-party services they choose to use or interact with.

06

Your cookie choices and controls

Depending on your location, the technologies in use, and the controls made available by BOMO, you may have options to manage certain non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

BOMO cookie controls

Where a cookie preference or consent tool is available, you may use it to review or change available choices for non-essential technologies.

Browser controls

Most browsers allow users to view, block, restrict, or delete cookies through browser settings. Available controls vary by browser and device.

Blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect your experience. Some platform functions may require essential technologies to maintain sessions, process requests, secure accounts, or provide requested services.

07

How long cookies may remain

The duration of a cookie or similar technology depends on its purpose and configuration.

Session technologies

Generally operate for the duration of a browser or application session and may expire when the session ends.

Persistent technologies

May remain for a defined period or until deleted, depending on their purpose, configuration, and applicable requirements.

Relationship with the Privacy Policy

Information collected through cookies and similar technologies may constitute personal information or personal data under applicable laws. Where applicable, BOMO's collection and processing of such information is also governed by the BOMO Privacy Policy.
