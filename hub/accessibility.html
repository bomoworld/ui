ACCESSIBILITY AT BOMO

Making travel information easier to access and use.

BOMO aims to create digital experiences that are clear, understandable, navigable, and usable by as many travelers and property partners as reasonably possible.

OUR APPROACH

Accessibility is part of a better travel experience.

Travel platforms contain important information about properties, bookings, prices, policies, payments, stays, and account activity. That information should be presented in a way that helps users find, understand, and act on it.

BOMO works toward improving accessibility across its public pages, traveler experiences, Property Partner tools, support resources, and informational content.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As BOMO develops new features and improves existing experiences, accessibility considerations are intended to remain part of design, content, development, and review.

ACCESSIBILITY PRINCIPLES

How we approach accessible digital experiences.

BOMO focuses on practical improvements that help users perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with platform information.

Clear Structure

Pages should use meaningful headings, logical content order, consistent navigation, and clearly grouped information.

Keyboard Navigation

Interactive experiences should support common keyboard navigation patterns where applicable, including access to links, buttons, forms, and important controls.

Readable Content

Typography, spacing, visual hierarchy, and contrast should help users read and understand important travel and account information.

Responsive Experiences

BOMO experiences are designed to adapt across supported screen sizes so important information and actions remain accessible on different devices.

Understandable Information

Labels, instructions, booking conditions, policies, and support information should use clear and understandable language whenever possible.

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility issues can change as products and technology evolve. BOMO aims to review concerns and improve experiences over time.

WHERE ACCESSIBILITY MATTERS

Across the complete BOMO journey.

Accessibility considerations extend across the different ways travelers and property partners interact with BOMO.

Property Discovery

Search results, property information, room options, pricing, and booking conditions should be organized for easier understanding.

Booking & Trip Management

Important reservation information, dates, statuses, policies, and available actions should be presented clearly.

Reviews & Participation

Review eligibility, upload requirements, statuses, and reward information should be communicated in an understandable way.

Property Partner Tools

Property management workflows should use consistent navigation, clear labels, understandable statuses, and structured forms.

Using BOMO with assistive technology

Users may access BOMO using different browsers, devices, operating systems, accessibility settings, and assistive technologies. Compatibility can vary depending on the technology and the specific platform feature being used.

Keeping browsers, operating systems, and assistive technologies updated may provide a better experience with modern web features.

Personal accessibility settings

Many devices and browsers provide built-in accessibility options, including text scaling, zoom, contrast preferences, keyboard navigation, screen readers, voice control, and other assistive features.

These settings are controlled by the user's device, browser, or assistive technology and may help personalize the BOMO experience.

ACCESSIBLE HOSPITALITY INFORMATION

Digital accessibility and property accessibility are different.

This page describes BOMO's approach to the accessibility of its digital information and platform experiences. Accessibility features at a hotel, resort, accommodation, or other property are provided and managed by the individual property partner.

Travelers who require specific accessibility features should review the available property information and, when necessary, confirm requirements directly with the property before booking or arrival.

Before booking

If an accessibility feature is essential to your stay, verify the property's available facilities and services before completing your booking.

ACCESSIBILITY FEEDBACK

Having difficulty accessing BOMO?

If you experience an accessibility barrier or have difficulty accessing information or completing a task, contact BOMO Support. Providing the page, feature, device, browser, and a description of the issue can help us better understand the problem.

Contact Support Help Center

Our commitment to improvement

BOMO is an evolving platform. Accessibility practices, features, and compatibility may change as the platform develops. We aim to identify barriers, consider user feedback, and make reasonable improvements as part of ongoing product development.
