BOOKING SUPPORT

Have your booking details ready.

For an existing reservation, provide enough information to identify the booking and understand the concern.

View My Bookings

Booking reference

Provide the booking number or reference associated with the reservation.

Property and stay dates

Include the property name, check-in date, and check-out date.

Clear description of the concern

Explain what happened, what you expected, and what assistance you need.

Supporting screenshots, when useful

Screenshots may help explain an error or discrepancy. Remove or hide unnecessary sensitive information before sharing.

ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Protect your account information.

Support may need information to identify the account and understand the issue, but you should never share your password.

Account email

Provide the email address associated with your BOMO account when appropriate for account identification.

Never send your password

Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, recovery codes, or complete payment credentials in a support message.

Describe the access issue

Explain whether the issue involves sign-in, registration, password recovery, verification, or unexpected account activity.

Security concerns

If you suspect unauthorized access, secure your email account and change affected credentials as soon as possible.

PROPERTY PARTNER SUPPORT

Get help with property operations.

Property partners should identify the affected property, module, and transaction whenever possible.

Property information

Provide the property name and identify the affected room, rate plan, reservation, payout, user, or setting when applicable.

Affected module

Identify where the issue occurred, such as Calendar, Bookings, Rooms, Rate Plans, Payouts, Reviews, or Settings.

Evidence and screenshots

Include screenshots, error messages, and the steps that led to the issue when they can help reproduce the problem.

SAFETY & SECURITY

Is there an immediate danger or emergency?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services first. BOMO support is not a replacement for emergency responders.

BEFORE CONTACTING SUPPORT

A few details can make your request easier to understand.

Use one clear subject

Keep one support request focused on one primary concern.

Include relevant references

Add the booking reference, property, reservation, or affected module when applicable.

Explain what happened

Describe the issue, when it occurred, and the result you need.

Protect sensitive information

Never include passwords, one-time passwords, recovery codes, or complete payment credentials.
