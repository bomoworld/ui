What is a rate plan?

The offer and rules applied to an eligible room booking.

RRate plan

Controls eligibility, guest terms, overrides, and promos. It does not duplicate the room’s Base Rate.

ODate override

Automatically increases, decreases, or fixes the price for matching stay dates.

%Promo rate

Applies a guest discount when its code, stay dates, rooms, and minimum requirements match.

1
One booking uses exactly one rate plan.

That plan may then apply a maximum of one override and one promo.

Base prices come from Room Settings. The rate-plan workflow determines when a plan is eligible and how its selected override and promo affect that inherited price.

Status rules

Status controls whether the plan may be selected for a live booking.

Status Bookable? Use Required action
Draft No Incomplete setup or internal testing. Test and review before activation.
Active Yes, when eligible Currently available for matching bookings. Monitor conflicts and results.
Scheduled Yes, during validity Future or date-controlled offer. Start and end dates must be present and correct.
Paused No Temporary suspension without deleting setup. Reactivate only after reviewing rules.
Archived No Historical plan retained for reference. Create a new plan instead of reusing obsolete rules.
5

Application and priority rules

Keep plan selection simple enough to explain and audit.

Default fallback

Use one fallback plan for each room inventory group. It applies only if no conditional plan matches.

Conditional plan

Use for weekends, seasons, holidays, long stays, or advance-booking offers.

Smaller number applies first

Priority 10 is evaluated ahead of 20, 30, or 40.

Room eligibility

Select all rooms or at least one specific room type. Never save an empty selected-room list.

Date eligibility

End date cannot be earlier than start date. Scheduled plans require meaningful validity dates.

Stay requirements

Minimum advance days and minimum nights must be zero-or-more and one-or-more respectively.

Recommended priority bands

Priority Recommended use Example
10–19 Highest-specificity, limited-date plans. Christmas & New Year
20–29 Recurring weekly or high-demand plans. Weekend Escape
30–39 Negotiated or member plans. Corporate Preferred
40–89 General conditional offers. Early Bird — 30 Days
Fallback Default plan; not compared numerically. Flexible Rate
i
Booking Channel is not an input.

Current eligibility uses rooms, dates, booking lead time, stay length, and stay days only.

6

Guest-policy rules

Payment and cancellation terms must match what the guest sees at checkout.

Policy System behavior Control
Free cancellation Guest receives a full refund within the permitted period. Property must communicate the actual cancellation deadline.
Partial refund Refund varies according to cancellation timing. Refund wording must be clear before booking.
Non-refundable No refund after confirmation. Use clear guest-facing disclosure and require acknowledgement where applicable.
Full payment online Collect 100% when the guest books. No additional deposit input is needed.
Partial payment Collect the configured percentage at booking. Specify where the balance will be collected.
Pay at property Collect a required online deposit, then the balance at the property. Deposit must be between 1% and 99%.
!
Regulatory responsibility

Cancellation wording, deposits, refunds, taxes, payment collection, and displayed totals must comply with the property’s applicable local laws and payment-provider requirements.

7

Override and promo rules

Separate tables and actions make the two price-rule types easy to audit.

ODate override

Automatic price change for matching stay dates. It does not require a guest promo code.

%Promo rate

Guest discount with an optional code, minimum stay, and minimum booking value.

1Maximum application

One override and one promo may be applied after the winning rate plan is selected.

Multiple matching rules

Rule type Resolution options Recommended default
Override Highest priority, highest resulting rate, or lowest resulting rate. Highest priority only.
Promo Best discount or highest priority. Best eligible discount only.
Use clear names

Name the situation, such as “Weekend demand” or “Holiday package discount.” Keep the amount in its own field.

Use unique promo codes

Use uppercase codes that are easy to read and cannot be confused with another active promo.

Test boundary dates

Test the first eligible date, last eligible date, and one date outside the range.

8

Rules, controls, and operating guidance

Required product behavior and recommended management controls.

Platform rules

Exactly one rate plan

A booking cannot combine two rate plans.

One override maximum

Overlapping overrides must resolve to one applied rule.

One promo maximum

Overlapping promos must resolve to one applied discount.

Base Rate is inherited

Maintain the starting nightly price in Room Settings.

Deposit is conditional

The percentage input appears for Partial Payment and Pay at Property.

Inactive means unavailable

Draft, Paused, and Archived plans cannot be selected for live bookings.

Recommended operating controls

Do

  • Keep one clearly identified Default fallback.
  • Use consistent priority ranges.
  • Use Draft while configuring or testing.
  • Review the read-only report before activation.
  • Test eligible, overlapping, and ineligible examples.
  • Archive obsolete plans instead of deleting history.

Avoid

  • Creating two plans with identical eligibility and priority.
  • Using unclear names such as “Plan 1” or “Special.”
  • Duplicating Base Rate inside a plan.
  • Publishing a Pay at Property plan without a deposit value.
  • Overlapping many rules without testing resolution.
  • Including service charge or local tax in this setup.
₱
Charges intentionally excluded

Service charge and local tax are not configured here. VAT and platform fee are inherited values displayed only in simulator results.
